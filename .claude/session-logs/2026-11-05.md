## New feature: per-chore parent approval

Requested for bonus chores, so a kid can't just tap them for free points.

- `chores.requires_approval` ("Needs a parent's approval" checkbox in the chore form, 🙋 in
  Settings > Chores).
- Tapping such a chore on the kiosk creates a `chore_approval_requests` row (no points, no confetti).
  Points and period are fixed at request time, so a late approval still pays that day's rate and
  counts for that period.
- Pending requests count against `times_per_period`: completions + pending can't exceed the limit
  (enforced in `request_chore_approval` and by disabling the card). Denials free the slot.
- `decide_chore_approval`: Settings PIN or a registered device's approval key (same as redemptions);
  approval writes the completion + 'earned' transaction. `complete_chore` now refuses approval chores.
- Push `chore_approval_requested` via `send-push` -> `/approve-chore/:id` (one tap on a phone).
- `get_kiosk_board` dropped and recreated with `requires_approval` and `pending_count`.
- Shared `ApprovalDecision` component now backs both `RedemptionDecision` and `ChoreApprovalDecision`.
- Migrations 0036-0037 applied by the user, grants verified, `send-push` redeployed. Commits
  `b821da5`, `1c1e2d9`.

Follow-up fixes (`f2e527e`):
- The kiosk board used the device's approval key, so on the owner's registered desktop Approve skipped

- The "Waiting for a parent" chip stayed after approving because a second request was pending (several
