## New feature: school / vacation points modes

The app was built for summer break, when every day earned full points. During the school year, a
school-day chore shouldn't earn e.g. 2 hours of screen time.

Decisions (confirmed with user):
- School days earn a fraction of the chore's points, **rounded up** (so every chore is worth at least 1).
  Started at ⅕ and was changed to **⅓** the same day because ⅕ felt too steep.
- The **points day rolls over at 8pm local**: Friday after 8pm earns weekend points, Sunday after 8pm
  earns school-day points. Implemented as `((now() at time zone tz) + 4 hours)::date`.
- **Holiday** follows the same 8pm shift: checking it before 8pm on the holiday, or after 8pm the
  night before, marks that holiday. Only one `holiday_date` is stored and compared against the current
  points day, so it switches itself off with no cron job.
- The kiosk shows **adjusted points plus a day badge** (School day / Weekend / Holiday / Vacation).
- Parents can optionally set a **custom school-day value per chore**. If it's left blank, the
  automatic ⅓ applies.

Migrations (all applied to production by the user through the SQL Editor; the Supabase MCP connector
has no permission on this project):
- `0019_school_mode.sql`: `families.points_mode` ('vacation' | 'school', default 'vacation') and
  `families.holiday_date`. Adds the internal helper `family_points_day(family_id, at)` → mode, rate date,
  day kind and divisor; the RPCs `get_points_day()` and `set_holiday(p_on, p_pin)`, which checks the
  Settings PIN server-side when one is set; and updated `complete_chore` and `get_kiosk_board`
  (`chore_points` is now the day-adjusted value).
- `0020_lock_down_school_mode_grants.sql`: blocks `anon` and `public` from calling the new RPCs, and
  blocks every client role, `authenticated` included, from the internal helper (separate-migration
  pattern).
- `0021_school_day_divisor_3.sql`: changes the divisor from 5 to 3. `create or replace` keeps its
  locked-down permissions.
- `0022_chore_school_points.sql`: an optional `chores.school_points int check (> 0)`, used by
  `complete_chore` and `get_kiosk_board` on school days.

Client:
- `usePointsDay.ts`: `usePointsDay` (refetches every 60s to catch the 8pm rollover), `useSetHoliday`
  and `useSetPointsMode`.
- `KioskPage.tsx`: day badge, and a Holiday checkbox (school mode on a school day, or to undo a holiday)
  that opens a `HolidayModal` asking for the PIN, or a plain confirm if no PIN is set.
- `FamilyPage.tsx`: "Points mode" card with Vacation and School buttons, and an explanation.
- `ChoreForm.tsx`: side-by-side "Points (vacation / weekend)" and "School-day points" fields. The
  school field previews the automatic value as a placeholder; typing overrides it and clearing it reverts.
- `ChoresPage.tsx`: each chore now shows "N on school days" under its points.
- `src/lib/schoolPoints.ts`: client copy of the ⅓ rule, used for previews. Keep it in sync with the SQL.
